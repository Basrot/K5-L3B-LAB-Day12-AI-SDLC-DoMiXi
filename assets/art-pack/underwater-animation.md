## Prompt

Create an actual animation sprite sheet for a 2D underwater side scrolling game, 4 columns x 4 rows EXACTLY, 16 equal square cells, all frames at identical scale and same center anchor. Transparent background. Each cell contains ONLY same character Ut Sen, full body, no water surface, no pool, no scenic background, no ground, no words, no borders, no prop outside cell. Use attached reference identity: pale round face, sideways almond eyes, small smile, black cheek beauty mark, blonde fringe over brown bob, coral bow. Petite chibi adult wearing teal ao ba ba, coral belt, brown rolled trousers, bare feet, small wicker pouch attached firmly at waist. Bold brown outlines and simple flat clean cel shading, turquoise/coral/cream warm cartoon. ALL frames face RIGHT, swim completely submerged in open water without waterline; eyes open. Row1 DIVE: 4 consecutive frames body pitched diagonally head-down-right about 45 degrees, arms extend then sweep backward, alternating bent kick legs. Row2 SWIM: 4 consecutive frames horizontal rightward underwater breaststroke, arms outstretched forward, pulling outward, backward along torso, recover forward, legs kick alternately. Row3 UNDERWATER PICKUP: 4 consecutive frames angled forward down slightly reaching hand toward small golden shell at right of hand, grasp shell, bring to waist pouch, tuck into pouch. Row4 ASCEND: 4 consecutive frames head-up-right diagonal at 45 degrees arms reach then sweep, legs kick, no waterline. Character must be fully INSIDE each of 16 cells, consistent size and head features, avoid decorative sparkles. Large crisp sprite sheet landscape/square, genuinely transparent.
